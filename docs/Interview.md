# Interview Notes

1. K.Sai - Manager in Siddhartha Voluntary Blood Bank
- Key Learnings:
We spoke with one blood bank representative to understand their current blood management and coordination process.
We learned that blood banks rely on stock registers, computer records, phone calls and WhatsApp to manage blood availability and emergency requests.
We were surprised by the difficulty of finding compatible donors quickly, especially during emergencies and shortages of rare blood groups.
The interview reinforced our approach and highlighted the need for real-time blood stock availability and expiry alerts, which we can consider as future enhancements to Swastya.

2. N.Anand - Vasundhara Blood Bank
- Key Learnings:
We spoke with a blood bank representative to understand how they handle blood shortages and coordinate with other organizations.
We learned that maintaining sufficient blood stock is challenging because blood collection depends on voluntary donations and donor availability.
We understood that blood donation camps play an important role in maintaining blood supply and encouraging voluntary donation.
The interview highlighted the importance of organizing blood donation camps and maintaining better communication with donors.

3. Dr.Raghu - RR Speciality Clinic
- Key Learnings:
We spoke with a doctor to understand how blood requirements are handled during patient treatment.
We learned that arranging blood involves coordination between hospital staff, blood banks, and sometimes voluntary donors.
We understood that having information about suitable donors and available blood banks can help hospital staff coordinate more efficiently.
The interview reinforced the importance of a centralized platform for managing blood requests and communication.

4. Dr Sudhir - Leelavathi Hospital 
- Key Learnings
We spoke with a doctor about the difficulties faced while handling urgent blood requirements.
We learned that emergency blood requests require quick communication and timely responses from blood banks and donors.
We understood that delays can occur when the required blood group is unavailable and additional coordination is needed.
The interview highlighted the need for faster blood request coordination and clear request status updates.
